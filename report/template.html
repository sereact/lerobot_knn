LIBERO by Lookup

Is a diffusion policy just nearest-neighbour search with extra steps? We built the nearest-neighbour search without the extra steps — a frozen image encoder, the demonstrations as memory, and a weighted average of the action chunks that followed the closest frames — and tuned it on LIBERO.

Method

What the policy does

Nothing is trained. lerobot-knn-fit embeds each of the 273,465 demonstration frames with a frozen ImageNet ResNet‑18 (both cameras, 2×2 spatial grid of layer‑4 features), and stores those embeddings next to the proprioceptive state and the actions that followed.

At every step the policy embeds the live frame the same way, keeps only demonstrations of the prompted task (nearest CLIP prompt embedding), and ranks frames by a weighted sum of per-modality squared distances, each divided by its typical scale so the weights are comparable. It takes the closest frame of each of the k nearest demonstrations, soft-max weights them, and averages the action chunks that followed. Overlapping chunks from consecutive steps are blended ACT-style.

How many neighbours?

k = 1 replays the single nearest demonstration; larger k blends the action chunks of that many demonstrations (one frame each), softmax-weighted by distance.

Inside the retrieval

What the policy sees

Memory frames embedded in the policy's own weighted metric — squared distance on these maps' inputs is the retrieval distance — then projected to 2‑D. Each demonstration is a filament; suites and tasks separate cleanly, and the goal suite's tasks, which share one scene, start from a common region that only the prompt can split.

Protocol

How the numbers were produced

Search score vs. held-out score

Every configuration re-evaluated before the official run. The search score comes from the episodes the optimiser saw (LIBERO initial states 40–49); the held-out score from initial states 0–19 it never saw. Winners of a noisy search regress — the gap is the optimiser's curse, and the reason headline numbers come only from held-out and official runs.
